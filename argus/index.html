ARGUS

ARGUS follows Herculaneum scroll CT data from a volume to a surface to a detector output. At each step it records the inputs, how the output was produced, and where the process stopped.

Open the GitHub repository ↗

Explore the project

Built by DarthCeltic and clexious. The published project runs locally. You can try its synthetic fixtures or follow the documented PHerc0139 w016 public-data control with Villa and a separately obtained checkpoint. The release makes no claim of reading an unread scroll.
