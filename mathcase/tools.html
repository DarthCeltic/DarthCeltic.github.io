Why is a triangle unavoidable?

Pick any dot. At least three of its five lines share a color. Suppose that color is red.

Look at the three dots on the other ends of those red lines. If any two are joined by red, they make a red triangle with your first dot. Otherwise all three lines between them are blue, so those three dots make a blue triangle.

The same argument works with the colors reversed.

The checker looks for one triangle among the 20 triples of dots in your coloring. Ramsey’s theorem guarantees a one-color triangle in every red–blue coloring of six dots.

Erdős–Straus · FractionsCan three unit fractions add up to 4/21?

Try three denominators

A unit fraction has 1 on top. Choose three different bottom numbers and see whether the sum is exactly 4/21.

Example: 4/21 = 1/7 + 1/22 + 1/462.

Why the exact check works

4xyz = 21(xy + xz + yz)

For positive increasing denominators x, y, z, this integer equality is exactly equivalent to 4/21 = 1/x + 1/y + 1/z.

This checks one sum. See a formula for a whole family of denominators.

What does saving a result do?

You can download the inputs and result as a file or save a copy in this browser. The file includes a fingerprint so you can check whether the result changed. These calculations use exact arithmetic. For a mathematical proof, open the Lean workbench.
