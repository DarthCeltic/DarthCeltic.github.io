November 24, 2026 · A fixed-endpoint Collatz descent theorem

The Lean statement proves T23(n) < n for n ≥ 3 when at most 14 of the first 23 shortcut steps start odd. A shortcut step sends even n to n/2 and odd n to (3n+1)/2. In ordinary steps, the drop occurs after 23 + h steps, where h is the number of odd starts in those shortcuts. Earlier work already implies this result: Terras (1976, p. 252 and Theorem 1.2) gives a drop within 37 ordinary steps, and Rozier and Terracol (Theorem 5.1 and Table 1) imply the same fixed endpoint. MathCase records a formalization of a known consequence, not a first proof or a solution to the conjecture. Read the statement, example, and proof links; see the dated research timeline.
