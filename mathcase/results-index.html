November 24, 2026 · A fixed-endpoint Collatz descent theorem

The Lean statement proves the exact endpoint T23(n) < n when n ≥ 3 and the first 23 odd-to-odd steps contain at most 14 odd starts; its ordinary-step reading is a drop after 23 + h steps. Earlier work already implies this result: Terras (1976, p. 252 and Theorem 1.2) gives a drop within 37 ordinary steps, and Rozier and Terracol (Theorem 5.1 and Table 1) imply the same fixed endpoint. MathCase records a formalization of a known consequence, not a first proof of the result or a solution to the conjecture. Read the statement, example, and proof links; see the dated research timeline.

How to read a record

Research checks and provenance

“Lean checked” refers to the exact saved statement and stated environment or replay. “Recorded” means a result is preserved in a receipt or artifact; it does not imply a fresh independent run. “Independent review” is used only where the cited reviewer or separate checker is named and the scope is described. A source hash confirms file identity, not theorem correctness.
