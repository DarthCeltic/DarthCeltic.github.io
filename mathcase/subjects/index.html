What the labels mean

Checked case points to a bounded MathCase case with public evidence and stated scope.

Supporting artifact points to work that supports a subject connection or workflow claim. It may not be a standalone checked case in that subject.

Proposed is a topic and a suggested check. It has not been completed and verified as a public case. An em dash means no artifact is linked in this table.

A category can share a case with another category. Papers submitted to aiXiv have a separate publication record.
