Collatz · saved proof checks

Find a checked step in the number pattern

MathCase has a shelf of 4,262 formal statements about specific families of starting numbers. Search a name or phrase, read what one statement says, then open the Lean source and the matching receipt.

One example: every start of the form 32,768 × k + 127 becomes 19,683 × k + 77 after 24 steps. That result is smaller than the start. The letter k can be any natural number.

New here? Start with the bounded 23/14 explanation, then return here when you want to search the saved statements. The library matches exact text; it does not decide whether two proofs are mathematically equivalent.

This is a finite collection of checked families. It does not show that every Collatz sequence reaches 1.

Search the saved statements

Choose to load the pinned public records (about 8 MB). Then search by theorem name or words in the exact statement. This is ordinary text search: it does not decide whether two proofs mean the same thing or suggest new proofs.

The proof records are not loaded. No data is fetched until you choose the load button.

How these records connect

    These are exact shared source-file and dependency counts. They do not say that two statements are equivalent, reusable, or novel.

      What these records tell you

      What question?
      For a listed family of starting numbers, what exact value appears after a fixed number of Collatz steps, and is it smaller?
      What did Lean check?
      Each record is bound to one exact Lean statement, its source file, and the corresponding line in a saved replay receipt.
      How was it checked?
      These are individual theorem modules from a pinned replay. Each row records its axiom report and whether sorryAx appeared.
      What is still open?
      The statements have not each had an individual human wording review. The records do not prove that all positive starting numbers eventually reach 1.
      What a recorded Lean check means

      Lean checking means the proof term was accepted in the recorded, pinned replay environment for that module. It does not automatically establish novelty, an individual human wording review, or a general theorem beyond that exact statement.

      Dependency notes list imports and references to declarations local to the source module. They are not a normalized closure of every imported Lean constant.
