How to read a result

A check applies to one exact statement.

For the prime-divisibility example, the saved record includes an actual Lean rejection and a repaired candidate. That makes the proof process inspectable without turning a known theorem into a discovery claim.

In plain language

If a natural prime p divides n squared, then p divides n.

p prime, p ∣ n² ⟹ p ∣ n

What it proves: the exact formal theorem was accepted by the saved Lean check.
What it does not prove: novelty, independent review of every explanation, or open-problem capability.

New checked result · Nov 23, 2026

A precise Collatz pattern gives a checked way downhill.

If exactly 12 of the first 20 accelerated steps start with an odd number, then 32 ordinary Collatz steps end below the starting number. Lean proves this for every number in each matching residue class, and proves exactly 125,970 of the 1,048,576 classes match. This is a bounded result; it does not prove every Collatz sequence reaches 1. The earlier 2,652-family replay and finite Ramsey checks remain in the results record.
