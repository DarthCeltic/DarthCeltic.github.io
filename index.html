MathCase is an AI-assisted mathematics workbench. It turns a reviewed statement and a proof candidate into an inspectable record: the exact claim, what the model tried, what Lean accepted or rejected, and what the evidence does—and does not—establish.

A failed candidate stays in the record.

MathCase demonstrates the whole loop on a known theorem: a model's first proof was rejected, the Lean type mismatch was preserved, and a corrected candidate passed the saved Lean and axiom checks.

Reviewed formal statement

∀ p n : ℕ,
Prime(p) → p ∣ n² → p ∣ n

If a natural prime p divides n squared, then p divides n.

  1. 01

    State the claim

    The formal target includes the primality condition. That condition matters.

    Statement reviewed
  2. 02

    Keep the failed attempt

    The first candidate supplied h : p ∣ n² where Lean required hp : p.Prime. The rejection stays in the record.

    Rejected · preserved
  3. 03

    Check the repair

    The saved proof applies the known theorem with both hypotheses: by intro p n hp h; exact Nat.Prime.dvd_of_dvd_pow hp h.

    Saved Lean check passed

This verifies a known theorem for its formal statement. It is not a new theorem or a claim that MathCase has solved an open problem.
