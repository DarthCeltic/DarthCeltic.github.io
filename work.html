Internal finite-sample summary

Collatz: a 4,096-case structure sample.

The frozen sample used 4,096 unique odd residue classes left after an earlier finite screen. In the discovery half, 2,002 of 2,048 representatives reached a smaller value within 64 odd-to-odd steps. In the holdout half, 2,007 of 2,048 did so. One of three registered hypotheses passed; the sample did not yield a general rule.

97.754% / 97.998%discovery / holdout bounded-descent rates on these sampled representatives only

These finite rates are not a convergence theorem or a population-wide probability claim. The initial-valuation hypothesis failed because the earlier screen forced that statistic; the holdout confirms only the registered finite rate.

Internal bounded CPU campaign summary

Erdős–Straus: 831,860 exact finite witnesses.

The campaign checked a factor-conditioned subset of the n ≡ 1 (mod 12) residual cases through d = 10,000,000. It recorded 831,860 candidate rows and 831,860 exact Egyptian-fraction witnesses. All 427 base-search misses in the audited range resolved inside the fixed 65,536-offset extension cap.

831,860 / 831,860candidate rows with exact finite witnesses · bounded to the recorded filter and denominator range

The full Erdős–Straus conjecture remains open. This campaign established no new parameterized identity, makes no novelty claim, and says nothing about filtered-out cases or denominators beyond the cap.

What the offset and miss analysis found

The largest first-denominator offset in the audited runs was 35,498, leaving 30,038 below the fixed cap. Per-range maxima were noisy; their correlation with denominator range was weak in v9 alone (r = 0.067), so the sample gives no bound on future ranges. The 427 base-search misses shared n ≡ 1 (mod 24), which is imposed by the candidate filter; their factor patterns still varied. The analysis found no single new rule covering the misses.

The detailed manifests, witness streams, extension replays, and audit remain in the internal research checkout. This page summarizes the result but is not a public reproduction bundle.

System engineering snapshot

The research system around the proofs.

Knowledge graph

A local searchable index contains 4,294 result and attempt records, including checked statements, dependencies, axiom reports, source and receipt hashes, and preserved failures. The graph is owner-only and is not a public dataset.

Human-reviewed statement batch

Fourteen elementary statements have recorded M0 statement reviews. Nine have individual Lean passes; later batch entries and aggregate reviews are tracked separately. These are standard facts, not open-problem discoveries, and none is promoted to M4 by this page.

Transfer comparison

A library-versus-baseline protocol and held-out targets are staged. The comparison has not run, so MathCase does not yet claim its knowledge graph improves later proof work.

Research controller

The local evidence layer supports stable case identifiers, separate statuses, preserved failures, and pause/resume. Model-driven proposing and adversarial checking are not running in this local-only work period.

The index and underlying replay receipts are not bundled on this public site. The MathCase repository link in the header points to the public source tree; internal summaries are labeled here so readers can distinguish them.
