Three bounded composition attempts did not produce an accepted aggregate proof. No theorem was accepted, and no aggregate claim is made.

Internal finite-sample summary

Collatz: a 4,096-case structure sample

The frozen sample used 4,096 unique odd residue classes left after an earlier finite screen. In the discovery half, 2,002 of 2,048 representatives reached a smaller value within 64 odd-to-odd steps. In the holdout half, 2,007 of 2,048 did so. One of three registered hypotheses passed; the sample did not yield a general rule.

97.754% / 97.998%discovery / holdout bounded descent rates on these sampled representatives only

These are finite sample rates, not a convergence theorem or a population-wide probability claim. The initial-valuation hypothesis failed because the prior screen forces that statistic; the holdout confirms only the registered finite rate.

Internal bounded CPU campaign summary

Erdős–Straus: 831,860 exact finite witnesses

The campaign checked a factor-conditioned subset of the n ≡ 1 (mod 12) residual cases through d = 10,000,000. It recorded 831,860 candidate rows and 831,860 exact Egyptian-fraction witnesses. All 427 base-search misses in the audited 10-million range resolved inside the fixed 65,536-offset extension cap.

831,860 / 831,860candidate rows with exact finite witnesses · bounded to the recorded filter and denominator range

The full Erdős–Straus conjecture remains open. This campaign established no new parameterized identity, makes no novelty claim, and says nothing about filtered-out cases or denominators beyond the cap.

What the miss and offset analysis found

The largest first-denominator offset in the audited runs was 35,498, leaving 30,038 below the fixed 65,536 cap. Per-range maxima were noisy: their correlation with denominator range was weak in v9 alone (r = 0.067), so the sample gives no bound on future ranges. The 427 base-search misses shared n ≡ 1 (mod 24), but that residue is imposed by the candidate filter; their factor patterns still varied. The analysis found no single new rule covering the misses.

The detailed manifests, witness streams, extension replays, and audit remain in the internal research checkout; this page is a status summary, not a public reproduction bundle.

System engineering snapshot

More than a proof button

Knowledge graph

A local searchable index contains 4,294 result and attempt records, including checked statements, dependencies, axiom reports, source and receipt hashes, and failures. The graph itself is not yet a public dataset.

Reviewed theorem batch

Ten MathCase-authored statements have exact human statement reviews. Nine have recorded Lean passes; one remains candidate-only. These are standard facts, not open-problem discoveries, and none has been promoted to M4.

Transfer comparison

A library-versus-baseline protocol and held-out targets are staged. The comparison has not run, so MathCase does not yet claim that its knowledge graph improves later proof work.

Research controller

The local evidence layer supports stable case IDs, separated statuses, preserved failures, and pause/resume. Model-driven proposing and adversarial checking are not running in this local-only work period.
